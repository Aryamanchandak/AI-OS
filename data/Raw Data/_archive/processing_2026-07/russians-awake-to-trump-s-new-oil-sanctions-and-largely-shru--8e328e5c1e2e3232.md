- Category: #articles
- Summary: The U.S. imposed new sanctions on Russia’s biggest oil firms, targeting Rosneft and Lukoil. Russians mostly shrugged, saying the measures may hurt companies but are unlikely to change Mr. Putin’s war aims. Oil prices rose and experts said the impact will depend on enforcement and buyers’ reactions.
