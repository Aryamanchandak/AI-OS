The Rotations in more detail....

Investment Strategy

Reports to senior members of the Investment Strategy team, who are responsible for ensuring that the strategic asset allocation is appropriate, individual investments are evaluated carefully and the overall portfolio is optimally invested.

Support senior members in maintaining and evolving the medium-long term investment strategy for both the matching and growth portfolios.

Support the research and evaluation of new asset classes and funds, including manager selection and investment due diligence.

Working closely with our external asset managers to maximise our capital deployment towards appropriate illiquid investments.

Contribute to the preparation of internal papers & investment proposals to the Investment Committee, all whilst working closely with external asset managers and internal stakeholders across Capital Management, Finance, Legal and Risk.

Credit Research

During this rotation you will work with senior members of the credit research function, which is responsible for overseeing the team in assessing and reviewing all new investment opportunities (public and private, internally- and externally-managed) while monitoring existing holdings via engagement with internal (including Credit Committee) and external stakeholders (including external asset managers and, rating agencies).

Public Credit

The role reports to the Head of the Public Credit team and incorporates both Portfolio Management and Trading activities.

With approximately £13 billion in assets under management, the public credit portfolio's objective is to invest in Investment Grade Debt in both Developed and Emerging markets, including structured credit products.

This position requires collaboration with Credit Research Analysts to evaluate new investment opportunities in public credit, providing a top-down perspective on the portfolio to enhance the research team’s fundamental analysis and contribute to the Public Credit team's assessment of relative value in potential investments.

The role involves aiding in profit and revenue generation through optimizing existing trades, conducting relative value analysis across different bonds, and evaluating these against the company's internal standards.

Additionally, the role supports the team in maintaining productive relationships with banks and brokers to ensure high-quality transaction execution as well as insights into market trends and macroeconomic factors.

The role will encompass four core areas:

(1) Trading,

(2) Portfolio Management,

(3) Macro and Markets Insight, and

(4) Investment Projects.
