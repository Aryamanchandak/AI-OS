

Standard 6(A) : - Disclosure of Conflicts

Must make full disclosure of anything that might hamper, or impair independence and objectivity as well as interfere with normal duties to clients and employer and prospective. 

the disclosure should be prominent easy to understand and in clear language. 



Disclosure of broker-dealer market-making should also be disclosed clearly here. 

This includes bonus/compensation that might make somebody focus on the short term.

Promptly disclose and avoid any COI
