AI OS Stage Map

Capstone scope is frozen until submission (end Dec / Jan): rings 0–5 are the submission, the outer ring waits until after. Source of truth: plan/stages.md. The spine is in the centre. Each ring further out is the next stage, so you can only build a ring once everything inside it works. Each line is a contract, numbered I1 to I20. Tap a node for its plan and a stage name to see that ring. Tap a contract to see who provides it and who uses it.

Contracts

A contract is the agreed shape of what passes between two parts. Each one gets a contract test that the provider and every user must pass. I1 to I5 match plan/interfaces.md. I6 to I17 are new drafts for later stages and are marked Assumed.
