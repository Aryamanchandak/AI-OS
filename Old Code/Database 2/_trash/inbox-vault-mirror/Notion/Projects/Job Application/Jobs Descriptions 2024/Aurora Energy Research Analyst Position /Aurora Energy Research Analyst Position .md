

The Graduate Analyst Programme at Aurora offers a dynamic, structured 21-month experience for recent graduates, designed to develop skills in energy markets and consulting. Participants rotate through three departments—Advisory, Research, and an optional third rotation in Commercial or Software Solutions—allowing them to gain broad experience across the company. Aurora offers extensive training, mentorship, and the opportunity to grow into an industry expert. The programme promotes flexibility in working hours, and candidates should have strong analytical, quantitative, and communication skills. Aurora values diversity and offers a collaborative international work environment. Applications open September 2024, and the programme begins in September 2025.
