## Metadata
- Author: [[Jennifer Bender, Remy Briand, Dimitris Melas, Raman Aylur Subramanian]]
- Full Title: Foundations of Factor Investing
- Category: #articles
- Summary: Factor investing involves constructing indexes based on specific characteristics of securities to potentially achieve better risk-adjusted returns. Factors like value, momentum, low volatility, dividend yield, and quality have shown excess returns historically and can play a significant role in explaining mutual fund returns. Understanding factor investing can help institutional investors optimize their portfolios by managing exposure to these factors and potentially outperforming traditional market cap-weighted indexes.

## Highlights
- A factor can be thought of as any characteristic relating a group of securities that is important in explaining their return and risk. ([View Highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01k9bxvx9xvbrqdcaeeyam1mfz))
- A large body of academic research highlights that long term equity portfolio performance can be explained by factors. ([View Highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01k9bxwe1emwcjmtbsmx30nd2t))
- Certain factors have historically earned a long-term risk premium and represent exposure to systematic sources of risk. Factor investing is the investment process that aims to harvest these risk premia through exposure to factors ([View Highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01k9bxwy0934ttdmkb87pnnfa5))
- We currently identify six equity risk premia factors: Value, Low Size, Low Volatility, High Yield, Quality and Momentum ([View Highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01k9bxx1z78j0dtpq3q92sk244))
- Factor allocations can be implemented passively using factor indexes, which may bring potential cost savings to institutional investors. ([View Highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01k9by06th6kxbwtajswjfh23a))
- Furthermore, factor indexes bring transparency to factor allocations, which helps alleviate the well-known problem of manager style drift and has positive implications for risk management. ([View Highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01k9by0fmg86na3a5qxp50k6cz))
- Market cap weighted indexes are also the only reference for a truly passive, macro consistent, buy and hold investment strategy. They aim to capture the long term equity risk premium with structurally low turnover, very high trading liquidity and extremely large investment capacity ([View Highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01k9by1ezj7rgp8rxc9hr88kav))
- Investors must form their own belief about what explains the historical premium and whether it is likely to persist. ([View Highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01k9ebm3j5kgptzjhww5wh7677))
- Factor returns have also been highly cyclical. These systematic factors have been sensitive to macro- economic and market forces and have underperformed the overall market for long periods of time. ([View Highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01k9ebme9ec18nxtnxrqa4jdhe))
- Diversification across factors has historically reduced the length of these periods of underperformance. ([View Highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01k9ebmyj2g62wzs54h1p3f5m8))
