Lead Signal Policy · Highest priority

Following a fire tragedy in Malviya Nagar, Delhi authorities, including the MCD and Delhi home minister, will seal unauthorized commercial buildings lacking fire safety NOCs and other approvals, withdraw the Bed & Breakfast policy, and conduct a month-long drive to check fire safety compliance at all hotels and nursing homes.

Why it matters The sealing of unauthorized commercial buildings and the withdrawal of the Bed & Breakfast policy in Delhi will likely lead to demand compression for non-compliant properties, potentially creating a flight to quality for compliant assets and improving the alpha signal for well-managed, regulatory-adherent real estate developers and operators within the region.
