

Standard 2(A) :- Material Non-Public Information 



Employers or Employees who possess material non-public information, that affects a value of an investment cannot act on it or get others to act on it. (No trading on insider information, or getting others to trade on that inside information for you) 



information is material if disclosure of it, will affect the price of a stock/investment, or if an investor would like it to have an edge when making a decision about said stock/investment.



information is non-public until it has been widely made available to the market place. Analyst call is not public disclosure, while selective disclosure allows for insider trading. 



Mosiac theory :- Reaching an investment conclusion through perspective analysis of publc information and non material non public information is not a violation
