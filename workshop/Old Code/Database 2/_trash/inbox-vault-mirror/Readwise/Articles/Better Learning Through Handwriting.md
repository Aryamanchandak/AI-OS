# Better Learning Through Handwriting

![rw-book-cover](https://www.sciencedaily.com/images/scidaily-icon.png)

## Metadata
- Author: [[ScienceDaily]]
- Full Title: Better Learning Through Handwriting
- Category: #articles
- Summary: Writing by hand enhances the learning process by engaging multiple senses and motor actions that are different from typing on a keyboard. Research shows that handwriting activates different parts of the brain than typing does, contributing to better letter recognition. Anne Mangen emphasizes the importance of handwriting in the learning process and highlights the lack of focus on haptics in educational science. Collaboration between disciplines like psychology and neurophysiology sheds light on the significance of handwriting and its impact on learning and cognition.
- URL: https://www.sciencedaily.com/releases/2011/01/110119095458.htm
