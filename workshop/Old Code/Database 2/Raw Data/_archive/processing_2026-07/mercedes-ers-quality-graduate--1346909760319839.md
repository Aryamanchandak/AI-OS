

What can we offer you?

We can offer you the opportunity to work in a state of the art Research and Development environment and, supported by a wealth of expertise, you will take on the challenges faced when delivering championship-winning Formula 1 components, services and programmes. You will be exposed to a wide variety of stakeholders in Formula 1, offering you the opportunity to widen your knowledge of the F1 PU, advanced manufacturing methods in electronics, learn failure analysis methodology and effective communication, and master conflict resolving and management of requirements, processes and priorities. This is a fantastic opportunity to start your career in Formula 1 and to develop into a wider operations management role or engineering. You will also have the opportunity to work towards chartered status with the IET / IMechE and will be assigned a personal mentor who will support you throughout.
