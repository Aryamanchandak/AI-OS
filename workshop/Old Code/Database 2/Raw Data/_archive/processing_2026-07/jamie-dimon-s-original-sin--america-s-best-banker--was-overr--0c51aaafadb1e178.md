## Highlights
- Dimon is walking advertisement for random fortune in finance, getting credit for policies that came before him and for successes that proved short-lived. ([View Highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01kq7j4wtprx2ph71w0a82r339))
- When you’ve been called, in a span of six years, the world’s best and worst banker, it’s fair to say the pendulum is over-swinging in both directions. Dimon was probably overrated during the financial crisis. Today he is, simultaneously, less nefarious than his critics claim and less innocent than his defenders insist. ([View Highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01kq7j59r128avfvmx60rawxj9))
- JP Morgan executed complex deals with collateralized-debt obligations that were both morally questionable and financial disastrous ([View Highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01kq7j6gqkhz60yad90reef6je))
- The bank’s credit derivatives team was hesitant to get too involved with such deals, not just because of Dimon’s famous scruples, but because the team before Dimon’s arrival considered many of these trades too risky. ([View Highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01kq7j6anhfyd2vyjsmtbcseg2))
- JP Morgan had all the bargaining power in those days, as evidenced by the initial price paid for Bear Stearns ($2 per share, before a drafting error forced a renegotiation) and guarantees that shifted the downside asset risk to the New York Fed. The simple fact is that Dimon thought he could gobble up Bear Stearns and Washington Mutual on the cheap, and their legal liabilities turned out to be far more expensive than he knew. Dimon signed off on them then, and he's paying for them now. ([View Highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01kq7j7mqbymxbmm49p7n202xe))
- Dimon and JPMorgan were supposed to be the world's best finance team because they were so good at risk management. Today their risk-management skills speak for themselves. Just count the scandals: from the “London Whale”, to the money-laundering investigations, the Chinese corruption allegations, and, most notably, the risk management and compliance failures at the heart of the $13 billion settlement (including the failure to spot the liability risk carried by Bear Stearns and WaMu). ([View Highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01kq7j88jf3mhvpcvs0yhw86qb))
- JP Morgan's problem, however, is that it chose to embrace the Jamie Dimon aura, and now the entire board is committed to his candidacy for sainthood. They have too much invested to admit that he's just an ordinary mortal, and while his experience makes him less unqualified than most to run a monstrosity the size of JPMorgan, his overall record—a growing list of settlements, fines, and investigations—is disappointing at best. ([View Highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01kq7kzg6mkdpg884y644jvf8c))
- In the long view, Dimon was a key player in the construction of Citigroup, widely considered the epitome of everything that is wrong with a financial mega-conglomerate. Then he helped build JP Morgan Chase, the new poster child for too-big-to-manage super-banks. Really, this is America's best banker? ([View Highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01kq7kzm2gvvrp38d07hcmx1c1))
