## Highlights
- A recent [study](https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract_id=5082524) by Michael Gerlich “…revealed a significant negative correlation between frequent AI tool usage and critical thinking abilities.” Students are using generic AI chatbots like ChatGPT that are built as pure productivity tools and not purpose-built platforms for true learning. ([View Highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01kcrwv851xn30qbpq6dkzz074))
- Skipping the Mental Workout ([View Highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01kcrwvz2wxmvz0fg24n3rptg2))
- Like muscle, critical thinking atrophies without regular use, and today’s students are skipping the mental workout entirely. Right now, we have an entire generation of students that can make answers appear without any effort. ([View Highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01kcrww37wxj6wmpkpn74zjrcn))
- Browsing the internet for answers is similar to using a library; students must still search, interpret, and apply the information they find themselves to answer questions. AI, on the other hand, often hands over ready-made answers and replaces critical thinking with a simple copy and paste. ([View Highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01kcrwwgak4kfdd0wbeaakmw55))
