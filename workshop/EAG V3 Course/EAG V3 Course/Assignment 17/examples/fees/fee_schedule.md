## 1. Annual advisory fee

The annual advisory fee is calculated on assets under management (AUM) using
the following tiers. Each rate applies only to the portion of AUM inside its tier.

- On the first 1,000,000 of AUM: 1.00% per year
- On the next 4,000,000 of AUM (from 1,000,000 to 5,000,000): 0.75% per year
- On AUM above 5,000,000: 0.50% per year

## 2. Billing

Fees are billed quarterly in arrears. The quarterly fee is one quarter of the
annual advisory fee, calculated on the AUM at the quarter-end valuation date.

## 3. Minimum fee

A minimum fee of 2,500 per quarter applies. If the calculated quarterly fee is
lower than the minimum, the minimum fee is charged instead.

## 4. Rounding

All fees are rounded to the nearest 0.01, with halves rounded up.
