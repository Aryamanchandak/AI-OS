- Summary: Sustainable gardening means choosing everyday practices to protect the environment and future generations. Private gardening often focuses on individual actions, which can feel empowering but may ignore bigger political and social causes and create "toxic localism." Access to private gardens is unequal—young, renting, and marginalized people face barriers—so community approaches may be more inclusive.
