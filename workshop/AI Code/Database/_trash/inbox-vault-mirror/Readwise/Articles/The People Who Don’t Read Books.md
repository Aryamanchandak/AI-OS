## Metadata
- Author: [[Thomas Chatterton Williams]]
- Full Title: The People Who Don’t Read Books
- Category: #articles
- Summary: Some famous people proudly say they don’t read books, which shows a bigger problem with their character. Books take time and effort but offer deep knowledge and wisdom that quick sources like blogs or movies cannot. Rejecting books often means ignoring the value of learning from the past and true understanding.
