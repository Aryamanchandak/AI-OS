- Full Title: Trump Threatens to Resume Nuclear Weapons Testing, Minutes Before Xi Meeting
- Category: #articles
- Summary: Minutes before meeting Xi Jinping, President Trump said he would resume U.S. nuclear testing “on an equal basis.”  
His remark came as China and Russia expand and show new nuclear delivery systems.  
A U.S. test could spark a new arms race and prompt other countries to test too.
