   A: The eight well-documented factors the paper highlights are: Value, Momentum, Carry, Defensive (Low Risk), Quality (Profitability), Size, Volatility (or Low Volatility/Vol premia), and Liquidity/Illiquidity (often grouped with carry-like premia).
- Summary: This document reviews the facts and fictions of factor investing, which has been around for several decades and is backed by an enormous body of literature. The authors focus on the main factors that dominate the academic and practitioner landscape: value, momentum, carry, and defensive/quality. They address 10 claims about factor investing, providing evidence to support or refute each claim. The article concludes that a consistent and disciplined exposure to a well-diversified multifactor portfolio is hard to beat, and sticking with factor investing is worth it despite occasional periods of underperformance.
