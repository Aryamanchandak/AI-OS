**Annual Report FY2024-25 | Reviewed:** June 8, 2026  
**Auditor:** S. R. Batliboi & Associates LLP (EY network India) — **Unmodified (clean) opinion**  
**Framework:** *Financial Shenanigans*, 4th ed. (Schilit, Perler & Engelhart)

---

## Executive Summary

Nykaa's FY2025 Annual Report shows genuine operational improvement — revenue +24%, EBITDA +37%, PAT +81%. The clean audit opinion (S. R. Batliboi / EY India) and the broadly consistent GMV-to-revenue growth trajectories are reassuring. However, three forensic flags warrant investor scrutiny before treating the headline CFFO figure of ₹467 crores as durable:

1. **CFS-3 [HIGH]** — CFFO quality is materially lower than the headline. Two non-recurring items (DPO stretching + prior-year tax overpayment normalization) explain ~₹195 crores of the ₹466-crore CFFO. Sustainable CFFO is closer to ₹270–310 crores.
2. **KMS-1 [MEDIUM]** — Fashion vertical EBITDA losses presented at -8.3% of NSV vs. -14% of GAAP revenue; NSV denominator makes losses appear 41% smaller. GMV is the headline metric in investor materials even where it is 2× reported revenue.
3. **DTA Recognition [MEDIUM WATCH]** — ₹258 crores of deferred tax assets depend on future profitability of loss-making subsidiaries (Fashion: -₹124 crore EBIT; International: -₹29 crore EBIT). Auditors flagged this as a Key Audit Matter.

## Finding 1 — CFS-3: Unsustainable CFFO (HIGH CONFIDENCE)

**The flag:** CFFO jumped from ₹0.25 crores (FY2024) to ₹466.63 crores (FY2025) — a ₹466-crore swing — while net income grew only ₹32 crores (from ₹40 to ₹72).

**Root cause — two non-recurring drivers:**

### 1a. DPO Stretching (₹123.7 crores non-recurring boost)

| | FY2025 | FY2024 |
|--|--------|--------|
| Trade payables (BS) | ₹635 Cr | ₹387 Cr |
| Change (cash flow WC) | +₹245.8 Cr | +₹122.1 Cr |
| *Incremental stretch* | **+₹123.7 Cr** | — |
| Trade payables turnover | 9.2× | 11.8× |
| Implied DPO | **39.7 days** | **30.9 days** |

*Cross-check:* BS trade payables delta (₹248 Cr) ≈ CF adjustment (₹245.8 Cr) — confirmed.

Trade payables grew 64% while revenue grew 24.5% — a 40 pp spread. The entire reported improvement in "working capital days" (42→34 days, −8 days) is explained by this 8.7-day DPO stretch. Genuine inventory and receivables efficiency contributed nothing net.

Per CFS-3 mechanics: once DPO stabilizes, the annual CFFO contribution from further stretching disappears. If DPO returns toward FY2024 levels, CFFO will absorb a ₹248+ crore reversal.

### 1b. Prior-Year Tax Overpayment Normalization (₹71.5 crores one-time benefit)

| | FY2025 | FY2024 |
|--|--------|--------|
| Current tax expense (P&L) | ₹53.8 Cr | ₹24.9 Cr |
| Taxes actually paid (CF) | ₹59.7 Cr | ₹131.2 Cr |
| Overpayment vs. expense | ₹5.9 Cr over | **₹106.3 Cr over** |

FY2024's ₹131 crore tax payment was ₹106 crores above P&L expense — likely advance tax overpayments for prior assessment years — which depressed FY2024 CFFO to near zero. FY2025 normalized. This one-time reversal will not recur.

### CFFO Quality Waterfall

| Driver | Crores | Recurring? |
|--------|--------|------------|
| Operating profit improvement (genuine) | +₹122.5 | ✓ Yes |
| DPO stretch (incremental) | +₹123.7 | ✗ Non-recurring |
| Tax payment normalization | +₹71.5 | ✗ Non-recurring |
| Trade receivables (smaller YoY build) | +₹85.5 | ~ Partly |
| Inventory build (more than last year) | -₹30.1 | Varies |
| Other WC items (net) | +₹93.5 | Mixed |
| **CFFO reported** | **₹466.6** | |

**Sustainable CFFO estimate: ₹270–310 crores** (backing out DPO stretch and tax normalization). Still strong progress from the effectively-zero FY2024 base, but materially below the ₹467 headline.

**Management characterization:** The MDA says "A reduction of ₹21 crores in working capital, driven by efficient management, contributed positively." This is technically correct (net WC change = −₹21 Cr), but it obscures that the ₹245.8 crore payables expansion was offset by ₹217 crore inventory build, ₹83 crore GST/other assets build, and ₹4 crore receivables increase. Calling it "efficient management" while DPO stretched 8.7 days is incomplete.

## Finding 2 — KMS-1: Misleading Performance Metrics (MEDIUM CONFIDENCE)

### 2a. Fashion EBITDA: NSV Denominator Makes Losses Look Smaller

The company reports Fashion EBITDA margin at **-8.3% of NSV** (improved from -10.3% in FY2024). The segment note (Note 48) lets us compute GAAP-revenue-based margins:

| | FY2025 |
|--|--------|
| Fashion EBIT | -₹124.54 Cr |
| Fashion D&A | +₹30.50 Cr |
| Fashion EBITDA | **-₹94.0 Cr** |
| Fashion GAAP revenue | ₹674.69 Cr |
| EBITDA margin (GAAP revenue) | **-13.9%** |
| Implied NSV (at -8.3%) | ~₹1,133 Cr |
| NSV vs. GAAP revenue | ~1.68× |
| Company-reported margin | **-8.3% NSV** |

Using NSV (~₹1,133 Cr) rather than GAAP revenue (₹675 Cr) as denominator makes the EBITDA loss rate appear 41% smaller. NSV is economic value of goods sold — a reasonable internal unit-economics measure — but expressing EBITDA losses as % of NSV while the consolidated "6% EBITDA margin" uses GAAP revenue creates an inconsistent basis that disadvantages reader comparison.

*Groupon analogue (from ch13):* Groupon's CSOI excluded its two largest cost categories; here the denominator choice alone produces a ~500 bps margin improvement.

### 2b. GMV as Headline Metric

GMV (₹15,604 Cr) is featured prominently — page 1, CEO letter, all investor presentations — at 2× GAAP revenue (₹7,950 Cr). GMV grew 25.4% vs. GAAP revenue 24.5% this year, so there is no growing divergence between the two. The core concern is framing: a reader seeing ₹15,604 crores may reasonably anchor on a figure twice the size of the economic activity Nykaa actually captures as revenue.

*Mitigant:* GMV growth ≈ revenue growth (1 pp spread), and the company discloses the revenue figure clearly. This is industry-standard ecommerce reporting; it is a flag to be aware of rather than a red flag.

## Finding 3 — Deferred Tax Assets: Future Profitability Dependency (MEDIUM WATCH)

DTA of ₹257.95 crores was flagged by S. R. Batliboi as a **Key Audit Matter**, citing "significant judgement and estimates involved in management's assessment of the likelihood and magnitude of forecasting future taxable profits."

Context:
- Fashion segment EBIT: -₹124.5 crores (FY2025), -₹8.3% EBITDA of NSV
- International segment EBIT: -₹29.1 crores on only ₹24 crores of revenue
- Together, these two loss segments are burning ~₹154 crores of EBIT annually
- DTA recognition requires a credible future-profit roadmap for these subsidiaries

If Fashion's loss rate does not improve materially, or if future taxable profit projections are revised downward, a partial DTA write-down (EMS-5 type: hidden liability release) would reduce equity and earnings.

*This is not a current-year manipulation — it's a prospective risk. Track Fashion EBITDA trajectory quarterly.*

## Finding 4 — AAS-3: Inorganic Growth Undisclosed in "House of Nykaa" Headline (LOW / WATCH)

**Claim:** "House of Nykaa Beauty Brands' GMV grew by 55% YoY to ₹1,695 crores."

**Accounting fact:** Earth Rhythm (ERPL) was an associate until November 26, 2024, then became a subsidiary. Its FY2024 GMV was excluded from the prior-year base (not consolidated), but its FY2025 GMV (from Nov 26 onward) is included. ERPL contributed ₹8.59 crores in revenue for the ~4.5 months post-close — a very small absolute amount.

**Assessment:** ERPL's contribution is immaterial to the 55% GMV claim; the growth is predominantly organic Dot & Key + other owned brands (Dot & Key has been consolidated since FY2022). However, no organic/inorganic split is disclosed for the 55% figure, which is the disclosure standard required by AAS-3 diligence.

*Per ch17:* "True organic growth excludes all revenue from acquired entities for 12 months post-close." Nykaa does not provide this split. Given Earth Rhythm's small size, the impact is likely 2–3 pp of the 55% GMV growth at most, but the absence of disclosure is worth noting.

## Watch List for Next Reporting Period

1. **DPO trajectory** — If payables turnover stabilizes at 9.2× or falls further, CFFO boost disappears and cash conversion will decline. Watch quarterly payables balance vs. COGS.
2. **Fashion EBITDA improvement pace** — Path to profitability requires meaningful improvement from -₹94 crores EBITDA. Track quarterly against management's FY2030 targets.
3. **DTA utilization** — ₹258 crores recognized; any downward revision to subsidiary profit forecasts triggers reversal risk.
4. **Borrowings normalization** — ₹961 crores (up 41%); ensure growth-phase working capital loans do not become structural leverage.
5. **Earth Rhythm post-close growth** — Monitor whether ERPL's full-year contribution in FY2026 is disclosed separately to enable organic vs. inorganic comparison.

## Forensic Conclusion

Nykaa is a fundamentally sound business in its Beauty segment, with genuine revenue growth and improving unit economics. The earnings quality flags are real but not evidence of intentional manipulation:

- **CFS-3** is the sharpest flag: the working capital story that management tells ("efficient management") is partially a DPO stretch narrative. Investors who anchor CFFO at ₹467 crores for valuation purposes should discount to ₹270–310 crores to reflect sustainable cash generation.
- **KMS-1** is a metric framing issue: Fashion losses expressed at -8.3% NSV vs. -14% GAAP revenue, and GMV featured over revenue.
- The DTA is a prospective risk contingent on Fashion's profitability trajectory — not a current-year distortion.
