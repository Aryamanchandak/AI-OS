- Summary: The study by Raj Chetty measures social capital across U.S. ZIP codes and finds that economic connectedness (EC) is a strong predictor of upward economic mobility. It shows that people from different socioeconomic status (SES) groups are less likely to be friends, which impacts mobility rates in their areas. Unlike other social capital measures, EC consistently correlates with higher chances of economic advancement.
