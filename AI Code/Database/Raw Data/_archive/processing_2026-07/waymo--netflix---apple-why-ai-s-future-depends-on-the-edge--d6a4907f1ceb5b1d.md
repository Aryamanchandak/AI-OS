## Highlights
- For years, the cloud has dominated as the backbone of computing – centralising vast volumes of data and processing power in remote data centres. 
  But as the volume of connected devices continues to grow and applications demand instant responsiveness, the limits of distance and latency are getting in the way. ([View Highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01krxrzk6kds48jk7spgx7j796))
- This is why the new frontier is at the edge: with billions of intelligent end-points sitting closer to where data is created, decisions are made and actions happen in real time. ([View Highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01krxs00284zfcq4xs5kpne7kf))
- This is further propelled by the fact that today’s digital economy runs on immediacy. 
  A few milliseconds can separate a safe self-driving manoeuvre from a crash or be the difference between a seamless video stream and a frustrating buffer wheel. ([View Highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01krxs0f0s40a89y88tjf8tg1s))
- Edge systems achieve this responsiveness by blending the best of both worlds – using local processing for the time-critical, privacy-sensitive or high-bandwidth tasks, while still syncing with the cloud for model training, fleet coordination or large-scale analytics. ([View Highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01krxs0rhdptjg3y8xeg5crnbj))
- STL Partners predicts that the edge computing market will reach US$424bn by 2030 thanks to the ongoing boom in content delivery and computer vision, with connected vehicles, IoT devices and smart infrastructure also predicted to impact this upward trajectory. ([View Highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01krxs14eyjncfdfz17knr7gq3))
- “Achieving fully autonomous freeway operations is a profound engineering feat – easy to conceive, yet hard to truly master,” says Waymo Co-CEO Dmitri Dolgov. ([View Highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01krxs8dq2pg3190erhmwcwx89))
- “This milestone is a powerful testament to the maturity of our operations and technology.” ([View Highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01krxs8hbb5jt6dzsns4zv8yrb))
- Streaming a video without buffering? Thank edge computing. Netflix’s global dominance is built not just on content but on its Open Connect programme, a massive content delivery network (CDN) that is a model of edge architecture. ([View Highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01krxsqhx53khzwm1eyb81zw9y))
- This means that instead of serving every episode of Stranger Things from a few giant data centres, Netflix places its own red server boxes – called Open Connect Appliances (OCAs) – deep inside local Internet Service Provider (ISP) networks all over the world. ([View Highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01krxsr1fcwysakvtf2c75wzy9))
- “We developed an infrastructure that efficiently delivers quality entertainment to our members – no matter where they are around the world or what device they’re watching on – in partnership with internet service providers (ISPs) worldwide.
  When you press play, the video is streamed from an OCA in your city, not from a server across the globe, drastically reducing latency and ensuring a high-quality stream. ([View Highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01krxst1xfscp6es7maeddxqe8))
- So, in this instance, the cloud handles your login and recommendations, while the edge delivers the video. ([View Highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01krxst6bv20p87qgwn9932r9d))
- While the iPhone and iCloud are central to its ecosystem, the watch’s most critical functions are performed locally for two simple reasons: speed and privacy. ([View Highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01krxt0efcn30dc2yd4h70xke0))
- When the watch detects a hard fall or a car crash, for example, it must react instantly – it cannot afford a network delay to ask a cloud server for permission to call emergency services. ([View Highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01krxt0k5axsbab7b887k3apg8))
- Likewise, sensitive health data like your heart-rate rhythm (EKG) is processed directly on the S-series chip. 
  This philosophy is central to Apple’s new AI features. ([View Highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01krxt0qksd3yxmpv8q2feehcj))
