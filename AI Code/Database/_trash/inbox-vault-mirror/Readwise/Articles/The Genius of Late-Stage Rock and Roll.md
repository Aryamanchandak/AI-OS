- Take, for instance, that McCartney show. I went because he is 83 and I’d never seen a Beatle. In that regard, his presence would have been enough. Yet what I came away with was a set of deeper impressions, not least the sense that I was learning something fundamental about what it means to be human. Call it endurance; call it perseverance. Call it memory and loss. Late style represents neither a footnote nor an epilogue but rather—should an artist live long enough—a necessary point of evolution. It reflects our most essential reckoning: what we do in the face of the unforgiving knowledge that life comes to an end. ([View Highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01krdrb4ay85z88w6dq79b7pms))
