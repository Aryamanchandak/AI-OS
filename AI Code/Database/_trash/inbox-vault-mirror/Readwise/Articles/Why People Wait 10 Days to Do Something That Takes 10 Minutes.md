## Metadata
- Author: [[Amanda Mull]]
- Full Title: Why People Wait 10 Days to Do Something That Takes 10 Minutes
- Category: #articles
- Summary: People often procrastinate on simple household tasks, even though they would only take a few minutes to complete. This is because humans are prone to prioritizing instant gratification over long-term goals. The anxiety of not having done these tasks can build up and outweigh the annoyance of actually doing them. This cycle of procrastination is common among otherwise productive individuals.
